I actually think you're making the right decision by waiting.

A database is one of those projects that seems simple until you start building it. Then you realize it's really a collection of many smaller systems working together.

Imagine trying to build one today. You would quickly run into questions like:

* How do I store data permanently?
* How do I organize files on disk?
* How do I search millions of records efficiently?
* How do I prevent corruption if the computer loses power?
* How do two users update the same record safely?
* How do I parse SQL?
* How do indexes work?
* How do transactions work?
* How do I recover after a crash?

Those questions become much easier after you've built a few real applications.
